# Balls On The Gym Pipes

The PE teacher gave you 10 balls. Knock down the ones stuck on the pipes
in the gym roof, and try not to add to the pile.

Play it: https://milosarcade.com/gym-pipe-balls/

## How to play

- Move the mouse to aim.
- Hold the mouse button to charge a throw, and let go to throw.
- Hit a stuck ball to knock it down.
- A ball that lands softly on a pipe gets stuck.
- Rolling balls fall off the ends of pipes.

Your score is the balls you rescued minus the ones you got stuck.

## Files

- `index.html` is the whole game: HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file.

Your best scores are saved in the browser.
